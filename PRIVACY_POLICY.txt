PRIVACY POLICY
Daily Qoutes
Messenger Bot
Last updated: August 12, 2026


1. WHO WE ARE

This Privacy Policy explains how "Daily Qoutes" (the "Page") collects,
uses, and protects your information when you interact with the Page and
its automated customer assistant (the "Bot") on Facebook Messenger.

"Daily Qoutes" is a page that shares motivational quotes, videos, and
related inspirational content. The Bot is designed to answer your
questions about the Page, its content, and general inquiries.


2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you message the Page on Messenger, the Bot receives the following
limited information, which Facebook provides automatically:

Your Facebook user ID, used only to identify your chat thread.
Your publicly visible name and profile picture, as shown on Facebook.
The content of the messages you send to the Page, including text,
images, and attachments.
The time and date of your messages.

We do NOT ask for, and you should never send us via Messenger:

Passwords, PINs, or security codes.
Full credit or debit card numbers.
Government IDs, such as passports, national IDs, or driver licenses.
Banking or financial account credentials.


3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information above exclusively for:

Responding to your messages and answering your questions.
Improving the quality of the Bot's replies and our content.
Keeping a record of conversations so we can provide consistent support,
for example, if a human team member follows up.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third
parties for any purpose, including advertising.


4. AUTOMATED PROCESSING AND AI

Some replies are generated automatically by an AI assistant. When you
message the Page, the text of your message may be processed by the AI
service provider (Google Gemini) to generate a helpful reply. Your
message content is used only for the purpose of generating that reply
and is not used to train our own models.


5. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION

Conversation records are stored on secured servers and are kept only
for as long as needed to operate the Page and provide support. You may
ask us to delete your conversation history at any time, see Section 8
below for how to contact us.


6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Page is hosted on infrastructure provided by Facebook (Meta) and
third-party service providers, such as Google Gemini for AI replies
and our web hosting provider. These providers process your data only
as needed to run the Bot and in line with their own privacy policies.
Facebook's own data practices are governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Page is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do
not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you
believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us
and we will remove it promptly.


8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTACT

You have the right to:

Ask what information we hold about you.
Ask us to correct or delete your information.
Stop receiving automated replies at any time, by blocking the Page or
simply stopping messaging it.
